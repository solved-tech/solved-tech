## Rationale

**Goal:** Make a visitor want to write (book / enquire) after living one hard-light day in one place.  
**Framework:** AIDA through the rail. Attention = huge hour. Interest = denser noon. Desire = the log feels true. Action = Write: email as the last field note (no floating CTA chip; banned).  
**Sugarman:** each hour earns the next; field notes recapitulate before the ask.  
**Ogilvy / AP caption craft:** SHORT captions stay factual who/what/where/when. Persuasion lives in title, section heads, field notes, and the write line — not in lyric overlays.  
**Rule 13:** hour is the display; copy does not invent a hero+subhead+CTA. Title ≤ 6 words. Captions ≤ 40 words where budgeted.  
**Truth:** daytime-only window kept; Jimmy Elizarraras credit kept; sample labelling kept.
