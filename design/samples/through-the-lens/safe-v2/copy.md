## Rationale

**Goal:** Make a visitor want to book a sitting (email enquire), while the page still reads as a sitting record, not a marketing site.  
**Framework:** PAS inside the device. Problem implied by a weak portfolio (smiles, mosaics). Agitation = we refuse performance. Solution = the sitting note + email.  
**Sugarman:** take number → gaze → asked line → enquire. Each stage earns the next.  
**Ogilvy / VoC:** concrete studio nouns (take, gaze, soft key, grey seamless); Asked lines stay short and spoken.  
**Rule 13:** copy serves the life-size plate and huge take. No hero+subhead+CTA formula (banned in art direction). Enquire lives in the bar and the blank note.  
**Truth:** EXIF example label kept; Gustavo Fring / taras_chaban credit kept; sample labelling kept.
