## Concept (one sentence)
The site *is* a marked contact sheet: three film strips of near-identical bursts, grease-pencil keeps and rejects, and “Hold these frames” as the only ask — the story is the choosing.

## Why Contact Sheet won
Angle 1 (Leiter) failed translation twice (v2 Framer panes; v3 procedural glass). Angle 2 is photographer-native and harder to fake with soft shadows. Round 3 makes the object literal: sprockets, edge print, misregistration, cut frames, wax-like marks, loupe.

## Round 3 translation

| Ask | Response |
|-----|----------|
| Real sequence | Strips A/B/C = five near-dupe frames from one Unsplash source each (crop/tilt/exposure) |
| Physical film | Edge print (Kodak · Portra 400 · arrows · frame range), sprocket bars, black border, rotated/offset strips, last frames clipped |
| Grease pencil | Wobbly double-stroke circles, yellow star, crop-mark L, reject X; no UI chrome |

| Mobile | Horizontal strip scroll — pull film through a loupe |
