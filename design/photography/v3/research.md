## What v1 got wrong
Hits every ban: package/rate cards, filter chips, shortlist hearts, cream/rust wedding theme, hero+subhead+CTA. Negative research only.

## What v2 Direction 1 got wrong (concept salvageable, execution kill)
Per Design Researcher critique (~22/50):

1. **Flat colour slabs + soft-shadow floating photos** = Framer editorial portfolio preset — *not* Leiter.
2. Leiter is looking *through* obstruction so the subject arrives delayed. v2 glued a nice wedding photo onto a Pantone field.
3. **AI lyric captions** ("Drown the sun glass", "Hello is a sky file") — classic tell. Captions must be 3–5 concrete words or none.
4. Costume-party citation: naming *Early Color* without cropping, fogging, overlapping, or obstructing the plate.
5. Fake album gutters, booking pills, Mailchimp email strips.

## Translation table — Through the Pane v3

| Take from Leiter / Early Color (Steidl) | Refuse |
|----------------------------------------|--------|
| Delayed reveal: colour/glass *in front of* the face | Full-bleed flat colour sections as wallpaper |
| Foreground obstruction: wet glass, condensation, soft-focus colour mass | Soft-shadow floating photos on coloured slabs |
| Near-square / varied plate scale; almost no type between plates | Equal repeated modules; magazine gutter fake |
| Early Color palette as *photographic* masses (umbrella red, taxi amber, steel, snow) | Cream SaaS + rust Enquire theme |
| Enquiry as colophon — one sentence + mailto | Forms, SEND strips, booking pills |
| Captions: concrete craft words (rain on glass, doorway, hands, after) | Poetic AI lyric |
