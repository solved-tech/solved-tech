## Rationale

**Goal:** Make a visitor want to email Mara to hold frames — without turning the contact sheet into a wedding marketing template.  
**Framework:** PAS. Problem = endless shortlists and package grids. Agitate = hearts are not decisions. Solution = grease-pencil keeps + one email ask.  
**Sugarman:** mast names the object → strips show the choosing → 07B1 under the loupe → “Hold these frames” → mailto.  
**Ogilvy / VoC:** photographer nouns (roll, keep, soft, grease pencil, 07B1); first person without lyric filler.  
**Rule 13:** copy serves the marked contact sheet and names the hero keep **07B1**. No hero+subhead+CTA formula. No Sussex. No “want a site like this.”  
**4U ask:** “Hold these frames” is Useful, Unique to the object, Ultra-specific with frame IDs, Urgent only as a soft write-me.
