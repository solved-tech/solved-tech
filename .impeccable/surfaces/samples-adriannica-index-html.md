# Surface: Adrian Nica sample homepage

## Scope
Single-page Persuade homepage at `samples/adriannica/index.html` — a Solved Tech sample for the real Bucharest studio Adrian Nica. Not a replacement for adriannica.ro.

## Visitor mode
Persuade

## Audience & job
Couples in București and Ilfov deciding who will photograph and film the day. They need to recognise the work, trust the terms, and call.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the wedding day told as changing light — morning, ceremony, outside — while the photographer stays. It refuses the live site’s darkened hero, script wordmark, stat row, and package builder.

OWN-WORLD: Plaster ground (#d5ddd6), ink (#1c1612), muted bark (#5c4e3f). One filled call, door green (#1f3f3a) with door ink (#f6f1e8); on the evening field that call inverts to door-ink fill and evening text. Each hour is its own field: morning sage, ceremony candle, party leaf. Evening is the room after dark (#241f1c) with warm paper type. Besley for the name, the hours, and his sentence; Onest for the UI. No cards, no eyebrows, no numbers as decoration.

STORY: A visitor sees an undimmed courtyard photograph, reads who this is for, calls or writes on WhatsApp, then walks the day, the studio rates that are actually published, and the delivery terms.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky plaster bar with the name, three in-page links, and a filled call carrying 0723 986 982. Full-bleed courtyard photograph, bright, type never on the faces. Below it, on plaster: “Adrian Nica” at display scale on one line, one sentence of the offer, the same call, and a WhatsApp text link. Name, sentence, and call sit inside a 1440×900 viewport.

FORM: Hours of light (grounded candidate 4 of 7). seed:06bf4b892c9c540f. Raises: one photograph owns the viewport and the name sits off the faces (flagship); the call is the only filled control (teletext); his own sentence is set as a quotation (industrial); each hour is its own light (chromatophore).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
The courtyard frame drifts once, like the sun moving, and holds still for reduced motion.

## Constraints
Static HTML/CSS only (`script-src 'none'`). Romanian. Facts and photographs from the public site only: no invented wedding prices, no cart, no calendar. Christmas studio rates only as published (from 300 lei / from 400 lei). Footer states this is a sample, not the live site. Root DESIGN.md remains the Mara Ellison darkroom system; this surface does not replace it.
