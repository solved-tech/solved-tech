## Audience & job

A visitor who is made to look, one full-bleed plate at a time, and a Solved Tech reader checking that the page is an honest fictional sample.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Navigation is a deep zoom through a concrete gallery void. The page refuses the horizontal track, the portfolio menu, and the hero-plus-button.

OWN-WORLD: `--void #000000`, `--chalk #FFFFFF`, `--concrete #1A1A1A`. Tektur for stamps, serials, and the brand edge. System mono for the credit block. Square 52px difference reticle. No radius, no extra colour.

STORY: Land on brutalism, step the vertical well, switch series from the stamp rail, hold a plate in the invert-then-zoom dialog, then read the colophon.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One 100svh plate. Stamps sit on the bottom rail below 1440 and in the left column at 1440. `void / frame` is the right edge at 1440. The photograph is the page.

FORM: Brief-pinned STRETCH v2.1, The Zoom. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Memorable moment

The scale-only dive between photo plates, and the 26s land breathe on the opening monolith. Reduced motion swaps plates instantly and never inverts.
